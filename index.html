01 / ABOUT

Understanding the whole system.

My interest in computers goes beyond the screen.

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at Florida Polytechnic University with a strong interest in how computers work—both through software and through the physical hardware underneath.

I’m drawn to hands-on problem solving: building systems, working through troubleshooting challenges, and exploring how individual components fit together. Embedded systems, electronics, CAD, 3D printing, and AI are areas I want to keep exploring.

As Founder & President of Phoenix Hardware Club, I’m also interested in creating practical, approachable opportunities for students to learn, build, and solve problems together.
